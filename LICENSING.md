# ICS Research Studio — professional and company licensing

ICS Research Studio is developed by David Glowalla as a workbench for computational geometry, numerical experiments and research visualization.

## License options and term prices

Professional and company research licensing is being prepared with the following prices, excluding applicable taxes:

| License | 3 months | 6 months | 12 months |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Individual professional — 1 named user | EUR 900 | EUR 1,500 | EUR 2,400 |
| Company research team — up to 5 named users | EUR 2,250 | EUR 3,750 | EUR 6,000 |
| Larger deployment / OEM / integration | Separate quotation | Separate quotation | Separate quotation |

The individual option is for one named professional. The company option is for one legal entity and up to five named users. Both cover internal research and development and provide the same core edition.

The intended package includes an identified Studio build, documentation, updates released for the purchased edition during the selected 3-, 6- or 12-month term, and basic installation assistance. Custom development, training, integrations and guaranteed support response times are separately agreed. Renewal is opt-in; the next term's price is confirmed before purchase. No lifetime update commitment is included.

Customer-created research results can be published and commercially used, subject to any separately licensed supplied material. The standard license covers use of the proprietary Studio edition; it does not transfer ownership or include proprietary source, redistribution, OEM embedding or third-party hosted access.
