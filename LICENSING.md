# ICS Research Studio — annual licensing

ICS Research Studio is developed by David Glowalla as a workbench for computational geometry, numerical experiments and research visualization.

## Company research license

Annual company licensing is being prepared at a proposed price of **EUR 10,000 plus applicable taxes**, for one legal entity and up to five named users conducting internal research and development.

The intended package includes an identified Studio build, documentation, updates released for the purchased edition during the 12-month term, and basic installation assistance. Custom development, training, integrations and guaranteed support response times are separately agreed. Renewal is opt-in; the next term's price is confirmed before purchase. No lifetime update commitment is included.

Customer-created research results can be published and commercially used, subject to any separately licensed supplied material. The standard license covers use of the proprietary Studio edition; it does not transfer ownership or include proprietary source, redistribution, OEM embedding or third-party hosted access.

**[Request an annual company license](mailto:darklightblackwing@gmail.com?subject=ICS%20Research%20Studio%20annual%20company%20license)** with your organization, number of users, target platform and intended workflow. An order confirms the available build, terms, price, delivery and annual dates before payment. Customer download and automated checkout are not currently available through this repository.
