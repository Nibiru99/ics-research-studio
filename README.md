# ICS Research Studio

**A private workbench for computational geometry, numerical experiments and scientific visualization.**  
Developed by **David Glowalla**, independent researcher and research software developer.

**[Request a demonstration or company licensing discussion](mailto:darklightblackwing@gmail.com?subject=ICS%20Research%20Studio%20enquiry)** · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-glowalla-b42a59394/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Nibiru99)

## Explore, inspect and compare

The Studio brings geometric constructions, experiment navigation, saved evidence and interactive inspection into a shared research environment. Working views include simplex exploration, recorded field replay, numerical probes, 3D/ASCII displays and result exports. The surrounding research programme investigates reusable geometric templates, structured retrieval, dimensional representations and particle/field models.

Potential evaluation projects include repeated geometry workloads, high-dimensional construction and projection, structural candidate retrieval, and research visualization. A demonstration can focus on the capability relevant to your team, with its development status and measurement limits stated explicitly.
